Password Strength Checker

Check how strong a password is and why. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere.

  • At least 12 characters
  • Lowercase letters
  • Uppercase letters
  • Numbers
  • Symbols

How this works & FAQ

Strength is estimated from entropy: password length combined with how many different character types you use. The crack-time estimate assumes an offline attack at 10 billion guesses per second — a rough guide, not a guarantee.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser as you type; nothing is transmitted or logged.

Does this check against known leaked passwords?

No, it only estimates strength from length and character variety. A password could score well here and still appear in a breach list — using a password manager to generate unique passwords is safer.
