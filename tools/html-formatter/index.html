      How this works & FAQ
    
    

      
Paste HTML and click Beautify to indent nested elements, or Minify to strip whitespace between tags. Void elements like <br> and <img> are handled correctly and won't affect indentation.

      

        
Does this validate my HTML?
No, it only reformats layout — it doesn't check for missing tags or invalid nesting.

        
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. Formatting happens entirely in your browser with JavaScript.
