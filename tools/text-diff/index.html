How this works & FAQ

Paste the original text or code on the left and the changed version on the right, then click Compare. The two versions line up side by side with line numbers: removed lines are highlighted in red on the left, added lines in green on the right, and unchanged lines line up plainly on both sides.

Does this diff word-by-word?

No, it compares line by line — a single changed word will mark the whole line as removed on the left and re-added on the right.

Can I compare code, not just plain text?

Yes — it works on any line-based text, including source code, config files, JSON, or logs.
