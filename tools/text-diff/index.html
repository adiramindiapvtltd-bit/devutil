Text Diff Checker

Compare two blocks of text and see line-by-line differences.

How this works & FAQ

Paste the original text on the left and the changed version on the right, then click Compare. Removed lines are highlighted in red, added lines in green, unchanged lines are shown plainly.

Does this diff word-by-word?

No, it compares line by line — a single changed word will mark the whole line as removed and re-added.
