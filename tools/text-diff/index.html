How this works & FAQ

Paste the original text or code on the left and the changed version on the right, then click Compare. The two sides line up row by row, with a shaded filler where one side has no matching line — removed lines in red on the left, added lines in green on the right. Click Edit to go back to typing.

Does this diff word-by-word?

No, it compares line by line — a single changed word will mark the whole line as removed on the left and added on the right.

Can I compare code, not just plain text?

Yes — it works on any line-based text, including source code, config files, JSON, or logs.
